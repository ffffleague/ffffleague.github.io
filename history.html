FFFFL History

Fourteen completed seasons, championship runs, retirements, expansion, protests, questionable draft decisions and enough league drama to require its own historical record.

The Story of Florida's Finest

The Early Years

Florida's Finest Fantasy Football League began in 2012 and has evolved through multiple generations of owners, franchises and platforms. The modern FFFL record book preserves the statistics that remain reliably maintained, while the league's history extends beyond those numbers.

Former owners Tasha, Logan, Steve, Joe C and Pete were part of the league during its earlier years, but their statistics pre-date portions of the league's currently maintained record books. Their time in the FFFL remains part of league history even where complete statistical records no longer exist.

League Timeline

The defining seasons, champions and moments in FFFL history.

2012

The FFFL Begins

Florida's Finest Fantasy Football League begins its first season. Brady Gaga becomes the league's inaugural champion.

From the Archives

A surviving draft report card from the championship season gave Brady Gaga a B grade. Jonah proceeded to turn the middle-of-the-pack preseason evaluation into the first championship in FFFL history.

🏆 Champion: Brady Gaga
2013

Oreo Blizzard Takes the Throne

Joe's Oreo Blizzard captures the championship and begins the first back-to-back title run in league history.

From the Archives

The surviving 2013 Draft Report Card gave Oreo Blizzard the highest grade in the league. Joe backed up the preseason expectations by going on to win the FFFL championship that season.

🏆 Champion: Oreo Blizzard
2014

Back-to-Back — and an All-Time Draft Blunder

Oreo Blizzard repeats as FFFL champion. The season also produces one of the league's most memorable draft mistakes when Marc, owner of Romo Wittens His Panties, drafts David Wilson after Wilson had already suffered the neck injury that ended his NFL career.

🏆 Champion: Oreo Blizzard
2015

Knightmare Breaks Through

Knightmare wins its first FFFL championship.

🏆 Champion: Knightmare
2016

Dawg Pound Wins It All

James and Dawg Pound capture the FFFL championship.

From the Archives

A surviving photograph of the 2016 physical draft board preserves a snapshot of the FFFL's pre-Sleeper era, with a custom draft board featuring Knightmare's Jared in a photo shoot with The Yocum.

🏆 Champion: Dawg Pound
2017

An Inaugural-Season Championship

Show Me Your TDs enters the FFFL and immediately wins the championship in its inaugural season. Tim's title run is powered in part by a huge rookie season from Alvin Kamara, helping turn a first-year franchise into an instant FFFL champion. The season also sees Justin, owner of Thread The Needle, retire from the league following the evacuation caused by Hurricane Irma.

🏆 Champion: Show Me Your TDs
2018

Romo Wittens His Panties Breaks Through

Marc and Romo Wittens His Panties capture their first FFFL championship.

🏆 Champion: Romo Wittens His Panties
2019

Andrew Luck, Bacc Field and the Caucasians Protest

The 2019 draft produces one of the most infamous moments in league history when Tasha selects Andrew Luck, only for Luck to announce his retirement roughly 30 minutes later. Kevin takes over Tasha's franchise for the 2019 season, establishing Bacc Field. The ownership change kick-starts Josh's three-season Caucasians protest of the league. On the field, Go Luck Yourself captures its first FFFL championship.

🏆 Champion: Go Luck Yourself
2020

The Sleeper Era Begins

The FFFL leaves ESPN and moves to Sleeper for the 2020 season. Josh's Caucasians protest continues into its second year, while Romo Wittens His Panties captures its second championship in three seasons.

🏆 Champion: Romo Wittens His Panties
2021

Bacc Field Wins — Despite the Draft

The 2021 draft delivers two memorable moments. Justin trades up from the seventh pick to the third pick to select rookie running back Najee Harris. Later in the draft, Kevin selects J.K. Dobbins shortly after it had been announced that Dobbins had torn his ACL and would miss the entire season. Despite the mistake, Kevin and Bacc Field go on to win the FFFL championship. The season is also the final year in which Just Bobby is co-owned by Thomas and Jerry before Jerry assumes sole ownership, and it marks the third and final season of Josh's Caucasians protest.

🏆 Champion: Bacc Field
2022

Diggs Island Wins — Marc Retires

Diggs Island captures the championship. Following the season, Marc retires Romo Wittens His Panties from the FFFL and Dyl Pickle Six enters the league.

🏆 Champion: Diggs Island
2023

Go Luck Yourself Wins Again — Two Veterans Depart

Go Luck Yourself wins its second championship. Following the season, Joe's Oreo Blizzard and James' Dawg Pound retire from the league. Njigbas In Paris and Prison Jeff enter the FFFL.

🏆 Champion: Go Luck Yourself
2024

Caucasians Champion — The Champions Dinner Begins

The Caucasians win the 2024 FFFL championship. Following the season, the league holds its first Champions Dinner, beginning a new league tradition. Justin retires Thread The Needle for the second time, and Tommy Gunz enters the league.

🏆 Champion: Caucasians
2025

Knightmare Returns to the Top

Knightmare captures its second FFFL championship, ten seasons after winning its first.

🏆 Champion: Knightmare
2026

The Contenders Era

The Florida's Finest Contenders League launches as an eight-team companion league to the FFFL. The new league brings several familiar franchises back into the Florida's Finest universe, including Thread The Needle, Romo Wittens His Panties and Dawg Pound.
